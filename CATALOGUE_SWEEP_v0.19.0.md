## Classification and identities

The sections above are review groupings, not genre definitions. North African artists also appear in the Arabic/transregional grouping; Turkish music has its own tags and is not labelled Arabic. South Asian devotional music is distinct from Arabic song. Franco refers to Franco Luambo associated with TPOK Jazz. Dave is the UK rapper. Ice-T denotes the solo rap catalogue rather than Body Count. Solo artists and related groups remain separate entries. No alias-search implementation or artwork override was introduced.

## Evidence and confidence

All additions use medium-confidence editorial numerical profiles. Most profiles draw on music knowledge rather than fresh source verification. Successful checks:

- Anoushka Shankar official biography: https://www.anoushkashankar.com/ — sitar and classical/contemporary acoustic/electronic work.
- Kronos Quartet label biography: https://www.nonesuch.com/artists/kronos-quartet — contemporary string quartet repertoire and cross-cultural collaboration.

These sources support those artists only, not all 79 profiles or the numerical scores. Other attempted pages were unavailable or insufficient and are not cited as verification. Shared numeric scores are acceptable; different numbers alone are not evidence of profile quality.

## Remaining limitations

No audio analysis or listening validation was performed. No guarantee that artwork services resolve every ambiguous name correctly. Classical/game score profiles summarise varied works and arrangements. Existing legacy duplicates, aliases and profile issues are not silently merged or removed because personal data may use artist names as identifiers. The original programme's full genre-by-genre top-10/15 evidence matrix remains uncompleted; this release completes the practical broad expansion pass requested here.
