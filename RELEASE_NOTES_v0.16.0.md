# ATM Mobile v0.16.0

## Playlist identities and smarter Music For recommendations

- Split **Dancing… to Funk & Disco** into separate **Dancing… to Funk** and **Dancing… to Disco** options.
- Added the ATM mixtapes **Euroshock** and **Soft Focus**.
- Added clean YouTube Music and Spotify links for all four playlists.
- Added an editorial fingerprint layer to Music For ranking for these playlists. The existing scenario score remains the majority of the ranking, while playlist artists, style families, descriptive tags and sonic targets now refine the order and demote weak stylistic matches.
- Existing Music For profiles retain their previous ranking behaviour until their own playlist fingerprints are reviewed.

## Catalogue

- Removed Chris Brown, Bucks Fizz, Evita and Tiffny.
- Catalogue count is now **2,496 artists**.

## Preserved

- Personal data continues to use the stable localStorage key `atm-mobile-v01`.
- Existing favourites, Want to Explore entries, dislikes, notes and history remain compatible.
- Six-button bottom navigation and the established ATM visual design are unchanged.
- Techno, Drum & Bass and Garage have not been added as incomplete playlist entries; they remain queued for later curation.
