# ATM Mobile v0.17.8 — quality triage

Catalogue remains at 2,811 artists. No additions or removals.

Reviewed all 315 additions since v0.16.3. 195 artists share all 11 numeric traits across 44 groups. These profiles now carry Low profile confidence and their actual within-batch group size; the remaining profiles carry Medium confidence. Unsupported blanket verification claims have been replaced with explicit editorial-estimate wording. Specificity tie-break values are reduced conservatively; no claim of measured precision is made.

13 concrete field corrections are logged with before/after values in QUALITY_AUDIT.json. They include the Brandee Younger UK-family error, War Metal and first-wave misclassifications, anachronistic jazz families and non-artist similarity entries.

## Limits and remaining work

This is completed triage, not completed artist-by-artist listening validation. Every addition has a status in QUALITY_AUDIT.json. Shared vectors still need musical reassessment; they were not arbitrarily varied. Alias and era flags identify unresolved issues. No broad artist removal is warranted by this data-quality review alone.

## Compatibility

All 2,496 pre-expansion artist records and all artist names are unchanged. The localStorage key remains atm-mobile-v01. Engine, layout, navigation and playlist links are preserved. Version and service-worker cache advance together.
