# v0.19.4 — representative sound review

Reviewed the 146 broad-era flags. Assigned editorial reference periods to 136 profiles and retained 10 unresolved entries. Reference years are approximate matching anchors for representative sound, not career spans, debut dates, peak rankings, or independently verified facts. The displayed decade is explicitly labelled “reference sound”. No engine weights changed: era remains a small component.

Corrected three style profiles. The Supremes' Motown identity is supported by https://rockhall.com/inductees/supremes/ . The Bad Dreamers and Cosaquitos en Globo were incorrectly given generic avant-garde profiles: artist-published https://thebaddreamers.bandcamp.com/ and https://cosaquitosenglobo.bandcamp.com/album/recall support synth-pop/synthwave identities. Their replacement numeric profiles are Medium-confidence editorial estimates, not audio measurements. No confidence values were upgraded. Other era choices were editorial and have not each been researched against independent sources. The full exact changes are in PROFILE_CHANGES_v0.19.4.json.

Ministry already centres on industrial metal and needed no change. Prism's existing tags identify Japanese jazz fusion; WITCH's tags identify Zamrock. Neither was silently switched to a namesake.
