## Profile method and scope

Each addition has its own numeric trait vector, stylistic tags, descriptive profile and related artists. Scores are editorial estimates, with medium confidence, rather than audio analysis or independently validated weights. Era values represent a reference period, not lifespan or complete career dates. This is a focused batch, not a completed top-15 audit of every subgenre.
