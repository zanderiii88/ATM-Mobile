# ATM Mobile v0.17.3

## Blues and roots coverage audit

- Added 50 verified gaps spanning pre-war country blues, Delta, Piedmont, classic female blues, Chicago, West Side, Texas, Louisiana swamp blues, jump blues, hill-country blues and later revivalists.
- Each addition has an artist-specific sonic profile for Artist Match, Vibes and Mixtape recommendations.
- The selection favours historically important and stylistically distinctive artists rather than overloading the catalogue with modern blues-rock guitarists.
