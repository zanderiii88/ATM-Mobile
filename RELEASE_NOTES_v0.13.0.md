# ATM Mobile v0.13.0

## ATM playlists move to their own accounts

- Replaced all 11 Road Trip YouTube Music links with the new **Artists That Matter** account versions.
- Added the matching **Spotify** playlist for every Road Trip option.
- Playlist buttons now offer **YouTube Music** and **Spotify** side by side.

## Four new ATM playlist choices

Added a dedicated **ATM Playlists** group inside **Music For...**:

- **Head Nodding** — alternative rap, classic hip-hop and left-field beats, with ATM recommendations plus YouTube Music / Spotify links.
- **Pop Rocks** — hook-heavy pop-rock with an 80s, 90s and early-2000s lean, with ATM recommendations plus both playlist services.
- **Air Guitar** — guitar-heavy rock, riffs and solos, with ATM recommendations plus both playlist services.
- **Aotearoa Calling** — ATM's cross-genre New Zealand playlist on both services. This remains intentionally playlist-first rather than forcing a misleading sonic ranking across unrelated styles.

## Home and Guide

- Updated **What’s New** to reflect the move to dedicated ATM accounts and Spotify availability.
- Updated the Music For... intro and Guide to describe the two-service playlist setup.
- The v0.12.1 mobile scrolling/performance fixes and six-item bottom navigation are retained.

## Deliberately held back

- **Mosulu Memories** and **Ambience** have ATM-owned YouTube Music and Spotify versions, but are not exposed in the app yet while their eventual role/name/scenario is considered.

## Compatibility

- Catalogue remains at **2,500 artists**.
- Similarity model/catalogue data are unchanged from v0.12.1.
- Personal data still uses the stable localStorage key `atm-mobile-v01`, so favourites, Want to Explore, dislikes, notes and history carry forward.
