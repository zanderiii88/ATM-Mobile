# Artists That Matter Mobile v0.11.0

Released: 15 September 2026
Catalogue: 2,500 artists

## Headline feature: ATM Road Trip playlists

Music For... now includes 11 dedicated Road Trip modes. Each one has its own ATM recommendation profile plus a direct link to a public ATM-curated playlist on YouTube Music.

Road Trip modes:
- Pedal 2 Metal
- Rockit
- Rap It Up
- Pop The Hood
- Cruise Control
- Sunset Strip
- Country Miles
- Autobahn
- Scenic Route
- Backseat Karaoke
- Nu Car

Playlist support in this release is deliberately limited to Road Trip categories and YouTube Music only. Tracklists can continue to evolve without requiring an app update because ATM stores the public playlist links rather than the individual tracks.

## Home

- Added a compact highlighted **What's New** panel.
- For v0.11.0 the panel only promotes the new Road Trip playlist feature.
- The panel's action jumps directly to the Road Trips section under Music For....

## Music For...

- Added 11 genre/mood-specific Road Trip recommendation profiles.
- Road Trip result pages include a dedicated **Open in YouTube Music** playlist panel.
- Road Trip cards show when an ATM playlist is available.
- Ranked, Randomiser and Pick one remain available for the ATM-generated artist recommendations.
- Existing Music For... categories remain unchanged.

## Catalogue

Added two artists to bring the catalogue to exactly 2,500:
- 156/Silence
- Bosse-de-Nage

Both were added with artist-specific genre, mood, atmosphere and sonic profiles and were checked against the existing catalogue before insertion.

## Guide and compatibility

- Guide updated for Road Trip recommendations and YouTube Music playlist links.
- Personal storage key remains `atm-mobile-v01`, so existing favourites, Want To Explore entries, dislikes, notes and history remain compatible.
- Existing artwork resolution remains unchanged: known artwork map -> TheAudioDB -> YouTube fallback.

## Technical

- App version: 0.11.0
- Service-worker cache: `atm-mobile-v0110`
- Catalogue metadata updated to 2,500 artists.
- JavaScript and JSON validation completed before packaging.
