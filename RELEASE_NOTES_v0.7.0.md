# Artists That Matter (ATM) Mobile v0.7.0

Released: 14 September 2026

## Summary

v0.7.0 reorganises the ATM Mobile home screen around the four main ways to find music and adds a focused electronic/Kraftwerk-lineage catalogue expansion.

- Final catalogue: **2,297 artists**
- Artists added: **31**
- Artists removed: **0**
- Electronic expansion candidates already present / verified: **13**
- Duplicate additions: **0**

## Home screen

The home screen is now organised in three clear groups:

1. **Discover & Explore** — Artist Match, I’m Feeling Lucky, Vibes and Music For...
2. **Your Lists** — Want to Explore and Favourites
3. **Our Catalogue** — the current artist total

All four discovery cards use the same neutral ATM styling. Artist Match is no longer permanently highlighted with a red/purple gradient.

The **Artist Match** card uses a simple person → person matching symbol. **Music For...** uses a compact frying-pan, car and bed symbol to represent the different real-life contexts covered by the section.

The Guide has been updated to explain the revised home structure. Existing Vibes and Music For... behaviour is retained.

## Already present and not duplicated

Neu!, Cluster, DAF, Fad Gadget, The Human League, Art of Noise, Sparks, The Knife, Stereolab, Com Truise, Cybotron, Juan Atkins and Model 500 were all found in the complete v0.6.1 catalogue and retained as-is unless otherwise noted.

`Neu!` remains the existing catalogue spelling rather than creating a duplicate `NEU!` entry. Its curated Related field has been refreshed to point toward Harmonia, Can, Cluster and Brian Eno.

## Recommendation checks

The new profiles were differentiated rather than cloned. A Kraftwerk closest-match spot check now surfaces a useful mix including Giorgio Moroder, Yellow Magic Orchestra, Karl Bartos, John Foxx, Telex, Gary Numan, Art of Noise and Wolfgang Flür.
