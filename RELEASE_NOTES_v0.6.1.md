# Artists That Matter (ATM) Mobile v0.6.1

## Summary

v0.6.1 is a focused app refinement release. The catalogue remains at **2,266 artists** and the artist data is unchanged from v0.6.0.

## App changes

- Renamed **Discover** to **Artist Match** where users choose a reference artist and get related recommendations.
- Bottom navigation now uses **Match** and **Vibes** for clearer mobile labels.
- Added **Quick Vibes** above the existing detailed Vibes filters. The initial set is Dreamy, Warm & Soulful, Dark & Brooding, Euphoric, Nocturnal, Heavy, Bright & Playful, Rhythmic & Groovy, Strange & Experimental, and Acoustic & Intimate.
- Quick Vibes rank artists using the catalogue's mood/style language plus sonic-profile numbers, rather than relying on exact tag matches alone.
- The existing **Build your own** genre/style/mood filters remain available and unchanged in purpose.
- **Music For...** keeps the existing occasions but recommendation cards now give a short artist-specific reason for the fit.
- Updated the **Guide** for Artist Match, Quick Vibes and Music For... behaviour.
- Version display now shows **ATM Mobile v0.6.1 · 2,266 artists**.
- Updated `version.json`, app metadata and the service-worker cache (`atm-mobile-v061`).

## Personal data compatibility

The local storage key remains `atm-mobile-v01`, so existing favourites, Want to Explore entries, dislikes, notes and listening/recent history remain compatible with this release. Backup/restore also remains backwards-compatible with v0.6.0 backups.

## Catalogue check

No outstanding names remain from the previously approved addition lists after checking the complete v0.6.0 catalogue. In particular, **Hernán Cattáneo, Quivver, Pole Folder, Luke Chable, Omid 16B and Henry Saiz were already present in v0.6.0**. Phoenix is also already reconciled in the workbook from v0.6.0.
