# ATM Mobile v0.14.0

Released: 2026-09-17

## Usability cleanup
- Kept the six-button bottom navigation, including **Lucky**.
- Simplified **Home** by merging the introductory copy into the ATM hero, bringing discovery choices higher up the page, and removing version/export/install housekeeping from the main Home flow.
- Made **What’s New** dismissible.
- Renamed the curated collection heading from **ATM Playlists** to **ATM Mixtapes**.
- Consolidated Music For... into five clearer headings: **ATM Mixtapes**, **Road Trip**, **Dancing**, **Energy**, and **Moods & Moments**.
- Playlist-enabled headings appear first and are expanded by default; recommendation-only headings follow and start collapsed.
- Headings and the items inside each heading retain the agreed alphabetical ordering rules.
- Simplified **Vibes** so Quick Vibes stay prominent while the detailed genre/style/mood controls sit behind **Advanced: Build your own vibe**.
- Reworked the **Guide** into compact expandable help topics, with backup, update and artwork details moved into a lower technical section.

## Preserved
- Catalogue remains at **2,500 artists**.
- Similarity and recommendation logic are unchanged.
- All existing YouTube Music and Spotify playlist URLs are unchanged.
- The stable personal-data storage key remains `atm-mobile-v01`.
- v0.12.1 mobile performance improvements are retained.

## Not included yet
Shareable/deep artist and Music For... URLs remain deliberately deferred to a separate navigation-focused release.
